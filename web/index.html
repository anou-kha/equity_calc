Range notation

Each player is a range of possible hands; a known hand like AsKs is a range of one. Combine entries with commas. Hands that would use a board or dead card are left out automatically.

WriteMeans Hands
QQa pair6
AKs / AKo / AK suited / offsuit / both4 / 12 / 16

ATs+ATs, AJs, AQs, AKs16
JJ-99JJ, TT, 9918
A5s-A2sA5s, A4s, A3s, A2s 16
AhKhone specific hand1
random or anyany two cards 1,326

Cards are a rank (2–9, T, J, Q, K, A) and a suit (c, d, h, s), with or without spaces: Js Ts 2h or JsTs2h.
